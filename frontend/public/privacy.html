Privacy notice

Last updated 30 October 2026

This site is a portfolio with a chat assistant that answers questions about Aleksi Valta's background. It collects as little as it can to run that chat and keep it from being abused. It sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and runs no analytics.

Who is responsible

Aleksi Valta, Finland, is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: Valta93@hotmail.com.

What is collected and why

  • Your IP address. Used to rate-limit requests and to cap how much of the chat one visitor can use per day, so the site stays available and its costs stay bounded. The legal basis is legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and available (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Your chat messages. Each message you send, together with the conversation so far, is processed on this site's server to find relevant material, checked by a classifier for prompt-injection attempts, and sent to the LLM provider below to generate the reply. The legal basis is legitimate interest in answering the question you asked (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Please do not put sensitive personal information, about yourself or anyone else, into the chat.

How long it is kept

  • Your IP address and the chat usage counted against it are held only in the server's memory and discarded at midnight UTC. They are never written to disk or to logs.
  • This site does not store your messages. The conversation lives in your browser tab and is gone when you close or reload it. The LLM provider's retention is described below.
  • Technical logs, metrics and traces, which contain neither your IP address nor your messages, are deleted after at most 15 days.

Who else processes it

  • LLM provider: to be named before launch. Receives your messages to generate replies. Transfer basis: to be named. Use of your messages for training: to be named.
  • Hosting provider: to be named before launch. Runs the server, and so carries your IP address and messages over its network.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict its processing, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. Because your IP address is discarded daily and your messages are not stored here, there is usually nothing left to retrieve by the time you ask, but you can always write to the address above.

You can also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Finland that is the Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).
